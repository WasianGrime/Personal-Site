About me

Second-Year Informatics Student, IU Bloomington

Ty is a second-year Informatics student at Indiana University Bloomington's Luddy School of Informatics, Computing, and Engineering, minoring in Marketing and Human-Computer Interaction and Design.

As an Asian American student working toward a career in UI/UX design, he's drawn to the details that make software feel intuitive: how people move through an interface, and what makes an experience click. Marketing and HCI/d round out that lens, from understanding an audience to designing for how people actually behave.
